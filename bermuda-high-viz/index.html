Bermuda High Hurricane Steering

How atmospheric patterns protect Tampa Bay

The Bermuda High Effect

The Bermuda High (also called the Azores High) is a semi-permanent high-pressure system over the Atlantic Ocean. Its western edge creates clockwise steering currents that guide hurricanes entering the Gulf of Mexico.

Key Finding

The typical position of the Bermuda High creates steering flow that guides Gulf hurricanes toward the Florida Panhandle and Big Bend, rather than Tampa Bay.
